What Can Members Do?

A library member account provides access to several library services.

Request Books

Submit borrowing requests for books available in the library catalogue.

Manage Reservations

Create and review book reservations through your member account.

Track Borrowed Books

View your active loans, return dates, and previous borrowing activities.

View Fines and Payments

Check outstanding fines and review information about payment activities.

Receive Notifications

View notifications related to your borrowing requests and library activities.
