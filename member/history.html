About Borrowing History

  • Your borrowing history contains records of books issued to your account.
  • Returned books remain in your history for future reference.
  • Returned late records may be associated with fines.
  • Books that are still borrowed appear on your My Loans page.
  • You can review outstanding fines on your My Fines page.
