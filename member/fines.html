Fine and Payment Guidelines

  • Return borrowed books by their assigned due dates to avoid late-return fines.
  • Review your fine balance before submitting a payment.
  • A payment request is not considered completed until the system confirms the transaction.
  • Manual payments must be verified by authorized staff.
  • Never enter actual bank card details into this simulated payment system.
  • Contact the library if you believe a fine was recorded incorrectly.
