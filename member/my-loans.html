Understanding the Borrowing Process

  1. Submit a Request

    Browse the catalogue and submit a borrowing request for an available book.

  2. Wait for Approval

    A librarian reviews your request and approves or rejects it.

  3. Book Issuance

    After approval, the librarian issues the book. It then appears in your active loans.

  4. Return the Book

    Return the book on or before its due date to avoid overdue fines.

Important Reminders

  • Check your due dates regularly.
  • Return borrowed books on time.
  • An approved borrowing request is not the same as an issued loan.
  • Overdue books may result in fines according to library policy.
  • Check your notifications for updates about your borrowing requests.
