About Notifications

Notifications keep you informed about important activities related to your library account.

  • Borrowing notifications inform you about request approvals, rejections, and book issuance.
  • Due-date reminders help you return books on time.
  • Reservation notifications inform you about reservation status changes.
  • Fine notifications provide updates about outstanding balances and recorded payments.
  • Account notifications provide important information about your member account.
