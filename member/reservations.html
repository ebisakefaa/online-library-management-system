How Book Reservations Work

  1. Choose a Book

    Browse the library catalogue and select a book you want to reserve.

  2. Submit a Reservation

    Submit your reservation request through the library system.

  3. Wait for Review

    A librarian reviews your reservation and updates its status.

  4. Collect Your Book

    Follow the library's instructions when your reservation is ready.

Reservation Guidelines

  • You may cancel a reservation only when its current status and library policy allow cancellation.
  • Approved reservations may require assistance from a librarian before cancellation.
  • A fulfilled reservation cannot be cancelled.
  • Reservation approval does not automatically mean a book has been issued to you.
  • Check your notifications for reservation updates.
