Category Management Guidelines

  • Use clear and meaningful category names.
  • Avoid creating duplicate categories.
  • Assign each book to an appropriate category.
  • Keep category descriptions relevant and accurate.
  • Check whether a category contains books before attempting to delete it.
  • A category that is already assigned to books should not be deleted without first handling its related book records.
