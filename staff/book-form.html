Book Entry Guidelines

  • Enter the complete and correct book title.
  • Provide the author's name accurately.
  • Select the appropriate category for the book.
  • Enter the ISBN when it is available.
  • Provide the total number of physical copies registered in the library.
  • Keep book descriptions clear and relevant to the book's actual content.
  • When editing an existing book, ensure that changes to the total quantity do not conflict with copies currently on loan.
