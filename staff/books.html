Inventory Management Guidelines

  • Each book record represents a book title in the library catalogue.
  • Total copies represent the number of copies registered for a book title.
  • Available copies represent copies that can currently be requested by members.
  • Availability information must be updated when books are issued and returned.
  • Book records should not be deleted without considering their existing borrowing history and related records.
