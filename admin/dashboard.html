Administrator Responsibilities

Administrators oversee the main management functions of the library system.

  • Manage registered member accounts.
  • Create and manage librarian accounts.
  • Oversee book inventory and category management.
  • Monitor borrowing requests, loans, and returns.
  • Review outstanding fines and payment records.
  • Access reports about library operations.
  • Ensure that administrative actions are performed by authorized users.
