Member Account Management Guidelines

  • Keep member contact and account information accurate.
  • Review member account information before changing an account's status.
  • Suspend an account only when there is a valid administrative reason.
  • Reactivate suspended accounts when the relevant issue has been resolved.
  • Account status changes must be performed by authorized administrators.
  • Do not delete member records without considering their borrowing, reservation, fine, and payment history.
