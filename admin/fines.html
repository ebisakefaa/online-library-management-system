Fine and Payment Management Guidelines

  • Fine records should be associated with the relevant borrowing records.
  • Record manual payments only after the library has actually received the payment.
  • Do not record a payment that exceeds the outstanding fine balance.
  • Update the fine status according to the total amount paid.
  • Preserve payment transaction records for financial tracking and reporting.
  • Online payment records must be verified through the simulated process or sandbox integration before being marked as completed.
  • Never store payment card numbers or security codes in the library database.
