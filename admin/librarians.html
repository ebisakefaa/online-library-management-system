Librarian Account Management Guidelines

  • Create librarian accounts only for authorized library staff members.
  • Use a unique email address for each librarian account.
  • Ensure that account credentials are handled securely.
  • Suspend accounts when staff access should temporarily be disabled.
  • Reactivate suspended accounts only when authorization has been confirmed.
  • Do not permanently delete staff accounts without considering borrowing and other operational records associated with them.
  • Review account changes and restrict account-management operations to authorized administrators.
