Loan Management Guidelines

Follow these guidelines when issuing and monitoring borrowed books.

  • Issue a book only after its borrowing request has been approved.
  • Verify the member's identity before issuing the approved book.
  • Recheck the book's availability before creating a loan record.
  • Record the actual issue date when the book is handed to the member.
  • Calculate the due date according to the library's borrowing policy.
  • Keep loan records accurate and up to date.
  • Monitor overdue books and follow up according to library procedures.
  • Process returned books through the dedicated returns page.
  • Ensure that returned books update the correct inventory quantities.
