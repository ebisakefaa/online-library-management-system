Member Service Guidelines

Follow these guidelines when assisting library members.

  • Verify the member's identity before discussing private account or borrowing information.
  • Check the member's account status before processing a borrowing request.
  • Review active loans and outstanding fines when assisting with borrowing activities.
  • Keep member contact information confidential.
  • Do not suspend, reactivate, or permanently delete member accounts from the librarian area.
  • Refer administrative account-status changes to an authorized administrator.
  • Record borrowing and return activities through their dedicated operational pages.
