Reservation Management Guidelines

Follow these guidelines when reviewing and managing member reservations.

  • Confirm that the member has an active library account.
  • Verify that the requested book exists in the library catalogue.
  • Check the current availability of the requested book before making a decision.
  • Approve or reject only reservations that are still pending.
  • Update approved reservations when they have been completed according to library procedures.
  • Do not complete a reservation that has already been cancelled or fulfilled.
  • Keep reservation records linked to the relevant member and book.
  • Do not treat an approved reservation as an issued book or active loan.

Reservation Workflow

Understand how the reservation process works within the library system.

  1. Member Submits a Reservation

    A registered member selects a book and submits a reservation request through their account.

  2. Librarian Reviews the Reservation

    The librarian checks the member's account and the requested book information.

  3. Reservation Is Approved or Rejected

    The librarian records the decision and updates the reservation status.

  4. Reservation Is Completed

    When the reservation has been served according to library procedures, the librarian marks it as fulfilled.

  5. Borrowing Is Recorded Separately

    When a book is actually issued, the librarian records it through the normal borrowing and loan process.
