Librarian Responsibilities

Librarians support the daily activities and services of the library.

  • Review member borrowing requests and approve or reject them according to library rules.
  • Issue approved books and maintain accurate loan records.
  • Process returned books and update their availability.
  • Monitor overdue loans and apply the appropriate fine rules.
  • Manage book reservations and their statuses.
  • Assist members with account and borrowing information.
  • Record eligible manual fine payments and review payment information.
