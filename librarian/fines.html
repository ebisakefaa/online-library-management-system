Fine and Payment Management Guidelines

Follow these rules when reviewing fines and recording payments.

  • Fine records must be associated with the relevant borrowing records.
  • Verify a member's fine information before recording a payment.
  • Record manual payments only after the library has actually received the money.
  • Do not record a payment that exceeds the outstanding fine balance.
  • Recalculate the fine balance after every successful payment.
  • A fine should be marked as paid only when its outstanding balance is zero.
  • Preserve all payment transactions for financial tracking and reporting.
  • Verify simulated or sandbox online payment results before treating them as completed payments.
  • Never collect or store payment card numbers or security codes through this page.
