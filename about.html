About Our Library

Discover our purpose, our services, and how we make library resources easier to access and manage.

Welcome to Our Online Library

The Online Library Management System is a web-based platform designed to make accessing and managing library resources more convenient.

The system provides a central place where visitors can explore the available book catalogue, search for books, and learn about library services.

Registered members can use their accounts to submit borrowing requests, view their borrowing activities, manage reservations, and check information about fines and notifications.

Library staff can use the system to manage books, review borrowing requests, process returns, manage reservations, and support the daily activities of the library.

Our Mission and Vision

The principles that guide the purpose and development of our library system.

Our Mission

To provide a convenient and organized way for people to discover library resources, access books, and manage their borrowing activities through a simple digital platform.

Our Vision

To support learning, reading, and knowledge sharing through an accessible and well-organized digital library environment.

Our Objectives

The system is designed around the following objectives.

  • Improve Book Accessibility

    Allow visitors and members to browse and search the library catalogue conveniently.

  • Simplify Library Operations

    Provide staff with organized tools for managing books, members, borrowing requests, and returns.

  • Organize Borrowing Activities

    Support the process of requesting, approving, issuing, and returning borrowed books.

  • Support Member Services

    Allow members to manage their profiles, reservations, borrowing history, and fines.

  • Improve Information Management

    Maintain organized book and user information to support library management.

  • Provide Better Communication

    Help members stay informed through notifications related to their library activities.

How Our Library Works

Follow these steps to start using the library system.

  1. Explore the Catalogue

    Browse the available books and find resources that match your interests or learning needs.

    Explore Books

  2. Create a Member Account

    Register for a library account to access member services and borrowing features.

    Register Now

  3. Submit a Borrowing Request

    Log in to your account and submit a request for an available book.

    Member Login

  4. Wait for Staff Approval

    A librarian reviews your request. An approved request can proceed to the book issuing process.

  5. Borrow and Return Your Book

    Collect the approved book according to library procedures and return it by the specified due date.
