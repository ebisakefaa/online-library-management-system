Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about using our library system.

How can I find a book?

Visit the book catalogue to browse available books or search using a book title, author, or category.

Browse Books

How can I become a library member?

You can create a member account through the public registration page.

Register as a Member

How do I request a book?

Log in to your member account, find an available book, and submit a borrowing request. A librarian must approve the request.

Member Login

How can I check my outstanding fines?

Members can log in to their accounts to view their fines and payment information.

Access Your Account

What should I do if I cannot access my account?

Contact the library through the available contact channels for assistance with your account.
