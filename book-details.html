About This Book

Algorithms are fundamental to computer science and software development. This book introduces readers to the principles used to design, understand, and evaluate algorithms.

It explores topics such as sorting, searching, data structures, graph algorithms, and algorithm analysis. These topics provide a foundation for understanding how efficient computer solutions are developed.

The book can be useful for students, educators, and readers interested in computer science and algorithmic problem solving.
